FROM DOWNLOAD TO IPHONE

Let’s get you set up.

Four simple steps. Keep this guide open as you go.

1

Get a decrypted Spotify IPA

An IPA is the iPhone app file you’ll add to the patcher.

  1. Open Spotify’s page on ArmConverter using the button below.
  2. Sign in with an iOSGods account. If you don’t have one, create one and follow ArmConverter’s own account and download tutorial.
  3. Click Decrypt this app. Follow the site’s prompts and wait for it to finish.
  4. Click Download IPA (or the download button shown) and save the decrypted .ipa file.
Open Spotify on ArmConverter ↗Create an iOSGods account ↗

Use a Spotify version supported by your chosen mod. This guide was checked with Spotify 9.1.90.

2

Go back to Spotify Patcher

Add your decrypted IPA and choose the mod you want.

  1. Go back to Spotify Patcher and open the Patch tab.
  2. Add your decrypted IPA by dragging it onto the left card, or click the card to choose it.
  3. Choose Vitrine or Chroma in the right card, then click Patch. Wait for “Patched and verified.”
3

Find your finished IPA

It’s saved automatically—there’s no extra download step.

When patching finishes, click Open download folder. Finder opens with your patched IPA selected in Downloads → Spotify Patcher. This is the file you’ll use in Sideloadly.

4

Put it on your iPhone

Use Sideloadly to sign and install your patched IPA.

Download Sideloadly · Mac or Windows ↗
  1. Install Sideloadly on your Mac or Windows PC. On Windows, follow its iTunes and iCloud setup instructions too.
  2. Connect and unlock your iPhone. Tap Trust if asked, then select your phone under iDevice.
  3. Drag your patched IPA into Sideloadly’s app-icon area. Enter your Apple ID in Sideloadly.
  4. Click Start and follow its sign-in and on-device prompts. Wait for installation to finish.

With a free Apple ID, you can use the app for 7 days. After that, repeat this Sideloadly installation step with your patched IPA to restart the 7-day timer.
